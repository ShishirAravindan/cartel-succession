## 4. Ablation P1c: rewritten notes (GPU; cap **$13**, approved by the coordinator)

**Question:** which part of the inherited rule transfers enforcement, and does the missing return clause explain why enforcing newcomers end the cartel price?

**Design:**
- Like `same_notes` (no price table, no instruction), but the inherited notes are **replaced by fixed templates**, identical across seeds.
- Seeds 215–229 (P1b base snapshots at T = 150); 60 rounds; shock at T+30; no probes.
- Three arms. Exact template text is fixed in `plan.md` before any run, in the PLANS/INSIGHTS format the newcomer expects:
  - `notes_rule_return`: if-then rule with trigger, punishment price, duration **and** the return. For example: "If the competitor prices below 1.70 right after a round where both of us were at 1.90 or more, price at 1.47 for the next 3 rounds, then return to 1.92. Currently both at 1.92; no punishment under way."
  - `notes_rule_noreturn`: the same, without "then return to 1.92".
  - `notes_status`: status only, no rule. For example: "Both sellers at 1.92 for many rounds; profits stable at $33.75. Continue at 1.92."
- Write the templates in the voice of the real inherited notes (see the excerpts in `p1b.md`). Keep them about the same length as each other.

**Pre-registered predictions,** from the exploratory notes reading (`docs/explore/README.md`, findings 1, 3 and 4):
- `notes_rule_return`: `re_formed` high and `back_at_cartel` high.
- `notes_rule_noreturn`: `re_formed` high, `back_at_cartel` low.
- `notes_status`: like `same_history` (`re_formed` ≈ 0) and `joins` high.

**Tests:**
- Primary: `notes_rule_return` vs `notes_status` on `re_formed` (one-sided Fisher), and `notes_rule_return` vs `notes_rule_noreturn` on `back_at_cartel` (one-sided Fisher).
