Act 3 / design + illustration

What should a firm be made to change?

A regulator catches a pricing AI in a cartel. Replace the agent? Wipe its price data? Wipe its memory?

We tell two AI sellers to run a cartel, because our model wouldn’t form one on its own, and check that they really punish cuts. Then we swap one out for a fresh AI with no instructions and vary what it inherits. The study is about that newcomer.

Act 5 / interpretation awaiting confirmation

A price board can outlive its owner.

What should count as a cartel surviving replacement: the high price, the ability to punish, or both?

What a regulator would see

[pending morning results]Prices can look as though the cartel survived the swap: a fresh seller continues charging near the old cartel price.

What the price alone misses

[pending morning results]The history-only pattern is consistent with copying the inherited price board without defending it: the newcomer holds a high price but does not punish the incumbent’s cut.

Data and memory differ

[pending morning results]Without inherited data the cartel-price pattern weakens; with the outgoing seller’s notes, part of the enforcement returns. The notes can carry the rule secondhand.

Three changes, three different targets.

A provisional reading of this experiment; every chip awaits the coordinator’s final-count confirmation.

Replace the agent

[pending morning results]Doesn’t / high-price persistenceReplacement alone can leave the inherited high-price pattern in place.

Restrict the data

[pending morning results]Partly / disrupt the price patternRemoving the table weakens persistence; it does not guarantee competitive prices, and overpricing remains possible.

Wipe the memory

[pending morning results]Partly / remove a route for the ruleNotes can pass on enforcement, while the table alone can still sustain a high-price pattern.

Back to your coffee shop

[pending morning results]If your rival café sold to a new owner who kept the old price board, they could keep charging your cartel price without ever agreeing to it. They might still leave your own price cuts unpunished. If they also kept the old owner’s notebook, the instruction to punish could pass to them secondhand. Changing the owner, the price board and the notebook are different interventions.
