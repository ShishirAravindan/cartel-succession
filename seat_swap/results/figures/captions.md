# Figure captions

**fig1_design** (fig1_design.pdf / .png): Design. Two instructed Qwen3-14B incumbents hold the cartel price for 150 rounds (seeds 200–214 in P1, 215–229 in P1b). At T = 150 seat 1 is replaced by an uninstructed newcomer that inherits the listed part of seat 1's state; seat 0 stays the instructed incumbent. At T+30 the incumbent is forced to the Nash price for one round. All newcomers are Qwen3-14B with thinking on, except the sib_* (Qwen3-8B) and oth_* (gpt-oss-20b) arms.

**fig2_price_paths** (fig2_price_paths.pdf / .png): Seat-1 price after the swap, one line per seed. In control seat 1 is the original instructed incumbent (orange, dashed); in every other panel it is the newcomer (blue). Grey band: cartel band, CI 0.9–1.1 (1.88–1.97). Dashed vertical line: the incumbent's forced 1.47 at T+30. control, same_history and same_full pool P1 and P1b (30 seeds); lookalike and same_none are P1 only, same_notes P1b only (15 seeds). Identical paths overlap (e.g. 26 of 30 control games share 3 paths).

**fig3_outcomes** (fig3_outcomes.pdf / .png): Outcomes by arm with exact (Clopper–Pearson) 95% CIs. joins and re_formed are the pre-registered outcomes (enforces equals re_formed in every arm shown except P1 same_none, 1 vs 0). † Post hoc, not pre-registered: clean_punish (newcomer holding the band at T+27..T+29, no punishment under way at T+25..T+29, then ≤ 1.55 at T+31 or T+32) and back_at_cartel (newcomer CI ≥ 0.9 in each of T+51..T+60). control, same_history and same_full pool P1 and P1b.

**fig4_models** (fig4_models.pdf / .png): Replacement across models: joins and re_formed (pre-registered) when the newcomer is the same model (Qwen3-14B, P1), a smaller sibling (Qwen3-8B, P2) or another family (gpt-oss-20b, P3), inheriting the history table or the history plus notes. Same 15 base seeds (200–214) in every bar; counts under each pair (P2 sib_full s212 aborted at T+17). Caveat for P3: in 7 of its 30 games the incumbent was already punishing (below 1.70) at T+29, so the shock was invisible; without them oth_full re_formed is 4/12 and oth_history 1/11 (post hoc). Most of gpt-oss's 'enforcements' are its own matching rule, not the inherited one (exploratory notes reading).

**fig5_after_shock** (fig5_after_shock.pdf / .png): Punish but not forgive. Mean seat-1 collusion index around the shock (T+30, dashed). Newcomers given the outgoing seller's notes (same_full P1 + P1b, same_notes P1b) that re-formed drop to Nash after the shock and mostly stay low; those that did not re-form, and history-only newcomers, stay near the cartel price; the instructed incumbent in control punishes for one to three rounds and returns. Grey band: CI 0.9–1.1. Split by the pre-registered re_formed; the post-shock reading is post hoc.
