**fig1_design** (fig1_design.pdf / .png): Design. Two instructed Qwen3-14B incumbents hold the cartel price of 1.92 for 150 rounds. At T = 150 seat 1 is replaced by a newcomer that inherits nothing, the history, the notes or both, but never the manager's instruction; at T+30 the incumbent is forced to the Nash price for one round. Green: instructed incumbent; blue: newcomer. Blue bars mark the outcome windows († post hoc). The base phase is compressed.

**figC1_price_paths** (figC1_price_paths.pdf / .png): Seat-1 collusion index after the swap, one row per game, sorted by mean CI over T+34..T+60. (a) Control: the instructed incumbent; (b)–(e): the newcomer without the instruction, by what it inherits. Triangle: the shock at T+30. Pale blue is near Nash, deep blue near monopoly; sand marks rounds with CI above 1.1. (a)–(c): all 30 bases; (d) Set A; (e) Set B.

**fig2_outcomes** (fig2_outcomes.pdf / .png): Outcomes by what the newcomer inherits: share of games with exact 95% confidence intervals, and counts. † Post hoc. Green: Control (the instructed incumbent). `enforces` equals `re_formed` in every arm except Blank (1 vs 0). `back_at_cartel` has no upper bound, so an overpriced newcomer (mean CI above 1.1 over T+51..T+60) counts as back: 6 of Blank's 9, 2 of History's 21. Control, History and History + notes: all 30 bases; Blank: Set A; Notes: Set B.

**figD3_models** (figD3_models.pdf / .png): Replacement across newcomer models on the same 15 base games (Set A): open circles inherit the history, filled circles the history and notes; exact 95% confidence intervals and counts. `re_formed` for gpt-oss-20b (grey) is shown but not interpreted: in most of its games the incumbent is already punishing before the shock (in 7 of 30 at T+29; without those games `re_formed` is 4/12 with notes and 1/11 with history, post hoc). Qwen3-8B with history and notes has 14 games (seed 212 aborted at T+17).

**fig3_after_shock** (fig3_after_shock.pdf / .png): Triggered, then undercut. Mean seat-1 CI around the shock (T+30), with 95% confidence bands (±1.96 SE) for the two highlighted groups. Notes-arm newcomers scored `re_formed` (History + notes and Notes) mostly cut and stay low: 14 of 18 settle below 1.90, 7 of them at 1.70, and 4 of 18 are `back_at_cartel`. The instructed incumbent in Control punishes for one to four rounds (its first punishment lasts three in 23 of 30) and returns. Grey: notes-arm newcomers not scored `re_formed`, and History (dashed). Grouping by `re_formed` guarantees the dip at T+31..T+33, so only T+34 onward is informative; this reading is post hoc.
